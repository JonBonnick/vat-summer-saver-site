Eligibility

What qualifies for 5% — and what doesn't

Children's dine-in meals

Qualifies: fixed-price kids menu deals, individual items on a children's menu, non-alcoholic drinks included in a qualifying meal.

Excluded: takeaway and delivery, adult-menu items, shared meals, anything with alcohol.

Cinema & theatre

Qualifies: child tickets and family tickets sold as one package including at least one child admission.

Excluded: adult-only tickets, concessions, merchandise, booking fees.

Attraction admissions

Qualifies: entry to theme parks, water parks, fairs, circuses, museums, zoos, aquariums and soft play.

Excluded: pay-per-ride charges, sports participation, separate retail, parking and food.

Definitely 20%

Adult-menu food, takeaway and delivery, adult-only cinema or theatre tickets, sports facilities, pay-per-ride charges, general retail goods.

The maths

Why "subtract 15%" is wrong

The rate drops by 15 percentage points — but that's not 15% off the till price. Here's the working.

  1. 1

    The tempting shortcut

    The rate drops from 20% to 5% — 15 percentage points. So some people take 15% off the price they see on the till.

  2. 2

    What that gives you (wrong)

    £120 × 0.85 = £102 — too low. The right shortcut is 12.5% off: £120 × 0.875 = £105.

  3. 3

    Why 15% is the wrong number

    VAT is charged on the net price, not as a slice of the total. £120 is £100 net + £20 VAT. 15 percentage points off the rate is not 15% off the price.

  4. 4

    The correct method

    Strip the 20% VAT first: £120 ÷ 1.20 = £100. Add 5% VAT: £100 × 1.05 = £105. Summer price £105, saving £15.

  5. 5

    Or one multiplication

    Take 12.5% off — multiply by 0.875. Because 1.05 ÷ 1.20 = 0.875.

  6. 6

    Where 15 does come in

    The saving is 15% of the net price. Quick refund check: price × 15 ÷ 120 (e.g. £120 → £15).

FAQ

Common questions

What is the Great British Summer Savings VAT relief?

A temporary reduction of UK VAT from 20% to 5% on selected family-focused supplies between 25 June and 1 September 2026. It applies to children's dine-in meals, child or family cinema and theatre tickets, and admissions to attractions like theme parks, zoos and museums.

Can I just subtract 15% from the till price?

No. The rate drops by 15 percentage points, but that is not 15% off the price. The correct adjustment on a price that includes 20% VAT is to multiply by 0.875 (12.5% off) — because 1.05 ÷ 1.20 = 0.875.

Are takeaway meals included?

No. Only dine-in children's meals from the children's menu qualify. Takeaway and delivery are excluded.

Do adult cinema tickets qualify?

Only child tickets and family tickets (sold as one package including at least one child admission) qualify. Adult-only tickets stay at 20% VAT.
